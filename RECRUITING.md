# Recruiting the first members: 50 personas

> **Internal planning notes.** These are fictional archetypes that help you decide **who to invite, where to find them and what to say**. They are not real people, and they must **never** be turned into accounts, profiles, posts or reviews. Every member of The Virtual Circle is a real, verified person; the only non-human voices are the clearly labeled 🤖 Circle Hosts (see [LAUNCH.md](LAUNCH.md)).

## How to use this

1. Each Friday, pick **one segment** (a few personas) for the week.
2. Send **10 personal invitations** using the outreach message as a starting point. Rewrite it in your own words and mention how you know them.
3. Invite only where you are already a member and the group allows it. Never spam, never pay for fake followers, never promise jobs or income.
4. When someone joins, walk them through the persona's **first week**. Ask your Founding Ambassadors to welcome them.
5. Track who joins by their referral code (Circle Ops → People) and note which messages work.

### 1. The Fresh Graduate With a Diploma and No Experience

*Aspiring VA - Philippines - fresh graduates*. Aged 21-23, recently graduated in business, communication, IT or education, living with family in a city or nearby town. Has been job hunting for months and finds that local entry-level jobs pay little and mean long commutes. Sees VA work as a way to earn from home but has no portfolio and no references.

**Goals**
- Land a first legitimate client within a few months
- Build a small portfolio and real references
- Contribute to family expenses
- Learn professional skills school did not teach

**Pains**
- Every posting asks for experience they do not have yet
- Cannot tell which online job offers are real
- Parents doubt that online work is a real career
- Has no idea what to charge, so is easy for middlemen to underpay

**Where to find them**
- University career fairs and job fair booths
- Student organisation and alumni groups online
- Beginner freelancing and VA groups on social media
- Campus career office webinars on remote work

**What will matter to them in the Circle**
- Circle Academy: structured lessons, quizzes and certificates give them something concrete to show clients
- Circle Bestie: practising real client situations, such as an upset client or scope creep, with an AI client before facing a real one
- Circle Jobs: only verified clients can post, and applying is free
- Circle Verified: an ID check helps a newcomer with no track record earn trust
- Circle Feed: seeing other people's first wins and questions makes the path feel real

**Outreach message** (make it your own)
> Hi! I'm the founder of The Virtual Circle, a new community for virtual assistants, starting in the Philippines that is just launching. If you're a fresh grad curious about VA work, you can join free: lessons, practice with an AI client, and job posts from verified clients only. No experience needed to start.

**Objections and honest answers**
- *"I have no experience, so what's the point?"* The Academy and Bestie were built for exactly this stage, so you can learn, practise and earn certificates first. We can't promise you a client, but you won't be starting alone, and applying to Circle Jobs is free.
- *"Is this another paid course in disguise?"* No. Tier 1 is free forever. Tier 2 Circle Pro is optional, and you can stay on the free tier for as long as you like.

**First week**
1. Join on Tier 1 and complete a basic profile
2. Complete the Circle Verified ID check
3. Finish the first Academy lesson and its quiz
4. Try one Circle Bestie role-play, such as The 3AM angry client
5. Post an introduction in the Circle Feed and ask one question

**Thriving looks like:** Finishes an Academy course with a certificate, applies to verified Circle Jobs posts, and shares their first client win in the Feed.

- Circle Jobs: direct applications to verified clients, free
- Circle Verified and Circle Talent: a way to document real work history and become visible to clients
- Circle Wellness: access to licensed practitioners for burnout

**Outreach message** (make it your own)
